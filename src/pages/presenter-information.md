### Poster Instructions

Paper authors and Doctoral Symposium participants are invited to present a poster during their designated poster session ([See Schedule](/poster-session/)). Posters should be:

- Printed vertically (portrait)
- Printed on thin paper
- Not laminated
- 24″ wide x 36 high (2 ft by 3 ft)
