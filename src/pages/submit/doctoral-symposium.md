### Call for Participation: AHLI CHIL 2027 Doctoral Symposium

The AHLI CHIL 2027 Doctoral Symposium offers PhD students an opportunity to present their ongoing or proposed research and receive valuable feedback from CHIL attendees and senior experts in the field. Participants will present their work in a poster session, fostering discussions and idea exchange, and a few will be selected for spotlight talks.

In addition to the poster session, we are offering a mentorship sessions during the conference, where selected participants will be able to discuss their research ideas and ask for career advice from senior mentors.
